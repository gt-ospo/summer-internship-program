# VSIP Schedule

This page has the latest schedule for the 2026 version of the Virtual Summer Internship Program. Please check this page for the latest update on when different training activities will occur and when milestone presentations will happen!

For the 2026 VSIP, sessions will happen at 11 AM ET on Tuesdays and 1 PM ET on Thursdays.

| Date      | Activity                        | Notes                                  |
|-----------|--------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Tuesdays  | Learning / Check-In Activities | All sessions will be recorded!         |
| Thursdays | Office Hours / Milestone Updates | Office hours are not recorded; general discussion |

> [!NOTE]
> Training schedule may change slightly during the program based on topic and trainer availability.
